# iPhone restaurant selection acceptance checklist

- Search by restaurant name, then clear the query and confirm the complete list returns.
- Select more than one price level; confirm combined results and the All reset.
- Apply cuisine, family, alcohol, ocean view, and entertainment filters independently and together.
- Open details, use Back, then Return to Hub without losing navigation.
- Confirm maps start collapsed; open a map, zoom in/out, then tap **Fit All**.
- With airplane mode enabled after initial installation, open a previously loaded restaurant and its street map.
- Verify address copying, favorites, sharing basket, and QR code on a narrow iPhone viewport.

Keep controls short and touch-friendly. Travel times are estimates, not measured routes (walking tolerance ~5 minutes, driving tolerance ~10 minutes).
