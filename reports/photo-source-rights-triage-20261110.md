# Photographic reference acquisition: source rights and image triage

Checked 2026-11-10. This is a research log, not a photo collection.

## Evidence and usage boundaries

- Cannery Row Press Room: https://canneryrow.com/press-room/ — media photo library states **editorial use only** with photographer credit. This is NOT blanket permission to redistribute images in an offline public dining PWA or to create derivative illustrations. Permission must be separately established.
- Jack's Bao operator: https://jackbao.com/ — confirms 751 Cannery Row, Suite 121; food images visible, but no verified entrance photograph or reuse license. Operator site is not itself permission to download or publish images.
- Cannery Row business listing: https://canneryrow.com/business/jacks-bao/ — corroborates business and address; the presence of an image is not evidence of rights or accurate current exterior depiction.
- Monterey County visitor directory: https://www.seemonterey.com/directory/jacks-bao/ — corroborates Suite 121 but lists a different phone than operator; prioritize operator for phone.
- 751 Cannery Row historic leasing flyer: https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/media.myelisting.com/listings/9/254467/monterey-canneryrow-flyer-GZy-E2X.pdf — suite 121 shown with a **former tenant**, not a current Jack's Bao entrance. Never use as current facade proof.

## Acquisition queue

1. Find a current, clearly identified entrance image, preferably from the restaurant or a photographer willing to grant explicit permission.
2. Record creator, original image URL, date captured (if known), address match, and written reuse/derivative permissions.
3. If permission is unknown, retain a URL and textual observations only; do not store the photo in the public repository.
4. Produce one realistic, original concept based on **multiple independently verified architectural facts**, not a direct imitation of a single unlicensed photograph.
5. Require visual acceptance before batch illustration generation. Reject generic cartoon storefront templates.
