# Illustration integrity policy (2026-11-10)

## Purpose
Use original illustrations to expand visual coverage without copying third-party photographs or implying that invented architecture is a verified entrance.

## Acceptance rules
1. Every illustration must be original and stored locally for offline access. Do not trace or closely recreate copyrighted third-party photographs.
2. Mark every conceptual image explicitly in the restaurant record's photoCaption and photoCaution. Do not describe it as a real storefront photo.
3. Maintain stable restaurant IDs, and keep photoCreator, photoSource, photoLicense, and photoDate fields populated.
4. Do not infer real signage, accessible entrances, building floors, hours, or open status from artwork.
5. Use restaurant-specific real-world architectural details only when independently documented; otherwise use an unmistakably generic conceptual scene.
6. Ensure images are cached by the existing service worker through each restaurant's photo field.
7. Never replace an authenticated licensed photograph with a concept illustration solely to increase illustration counts.
8. Do not claim an original illustration eliminates all legal concerns; logos, trademarks and copied trade dress may still require care.

## Current quality distinction
The first two batches use original **stylized vector** storefront concepts, not photorealistic location images. Future artwork should be evaluated separately for resemblance and navigational usefulness.
