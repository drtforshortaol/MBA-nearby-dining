1. Open the Hub's Volunteer Tools → Nearby Dining direct shortcut; confirm the restaurant search loads without an intermediate landing page.
2. Check that the Dining header displays the branded fork-and-knife icon and Return to Hub is enabled on the opening screen.
3. Search for “Archie”; confirm the single restaurant card appears among 209 visitor-eligible entries.
4. Open the restaurant card and verify the entrance photo and optional outdoor-seating photo after image assets are published.
5. Tap Return to Hub; verify the Hub opens.
6. Install Dining to the iPhone Home Screen via Safari; verify the installed icon separately from the in-app header.
7. Load Dining online, close it, enable airplane mode, reopen it and check restaurant search and locally cached images.
8. Check Updates and verify the displayed version matches the published build.

Note: the two owner-submitted Archie's JPEGs are not in GitHub yet; do not mark photo verification passed until binary assets are committed and referenced in data/eateries.json.
